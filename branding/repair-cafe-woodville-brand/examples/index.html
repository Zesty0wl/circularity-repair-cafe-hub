Do

Set body text in ink or navy. Use orange for buttons, icons, and highlights. Reverse to white on navy or on photos.

Don't

Set paragraphs or small labels in orange, sand, or stone on white. It fails contrast and gets hard to read.

03 · Typography

Hanken Grotesk

One family, two jobs. Titles are Black and UPPERCASE. Body is Light. The contrast between the two is the type personality, so keep it.

Brand quirk. In titles, write the numeral 1 as a capital I. So PART I, not "Part 1". Body copy and dates use normal numerals.

06 · Voice

How we sound

Warm, practical, and encouraging. We lead with what people get: new skills, money saved, less waste, good company. We never lecture.

Key messages

I.Repair Cafes are volunteer-run community events where you can fix your broken items with skilled local repairers, learning new skills as you go.

2.Every Repair Cafe is powered by volunteers, and we are always looking for new repairers to share their skills.

3.Repairing together saves money, reduces waste, keeps things in use for longer, and brings people together.

4.With more than 2,500 Repair Cafes worldwide, find your nearest event and help create a more circular future.

Do say

"Bring something to fix." "Learn as you go." "Everyone welcome." Specific, active, kind.

Don't say

"Stop being wasteful." Corporate hype. Anything with an em dash. Keep it human.
